# Light Bringer — Fact-Check

Data file: `src/data/lightBringer.ts`
Primary source: `Red Rising Books/Light Bringer.txt` (full novel; chapter boundaries indexed from the body headings — all 89 body headings match the TOC). Chapter numbers below refer to the novel's actual chapters (1–89). The novel has **no Prologue**.
Secondary: red-rising.fandom.com (WebSearch snippets only; used for alias cross-checks).

## Summary

- Entries checked: 20 characters, 1 chapter list (90 entries), 8 family groups.
- Errors found: 71 (HIGH 27 · MED 32 · LOW 12).
- Headline problems:
  1. The file invents a **Chapter 0 "Prologue"** that does not exist; 16 fields are keyed to chapter 0 and are therefore wrong/unreachable.
  2. **Cassius au Bellona's death** (ch 84, killed by Lysander) is missing entirely — he is listed alive with no entry after ch 35.
  3. **Atlas au Raa's death** (ch 84, shot by Lysander) is missing — one generic ch 5 entry only.
  4. **Ajax** is credited to Darrow at ch 55; he was killed by **Victra** (with Thraxa) on Phobos, revealed ch 30.
  5. **Volsung Fá** was killed by **Volga**, not Darrow (ch 76).
  6. **Apollonius** is placed in Darrow's crew from "ch 0"; in the book he is Darrow's enemy, holds Sevro captive, and fights for Lysander.
  7. **Sevro** is described as "with Virginia, tortured by Atlas"; he is actually Apollonius's prisoner at the Venus dockyards, rescued by Darrow in ch 9, and travels with Darrow the whole book.
  8. **Pytha** is Lysander's ship captain, not Cassius's companion.
  9. **Atalantia is Ajax's aunt, not his mother**; Ajax's parents are Aja au Grimmus and Atlas au Raa.
  10. The "Sack of Demeter" (ch 88) is Lysander looting Demeter's Garter on Io — Atalantia is not involved (0 mentions in ch 88).
